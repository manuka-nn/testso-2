A complete order, start to finish, recorded on a brand-new Seamline system: set-up → product → stock → quote → customer approval → order → live tracking link → invoice → payment → profit.

1First start

Open index.html with VS Code Live Server. The Seamline loader plays, then — because the system is empty — you create the workspace: company name, your name, email and password. You become the admin.

2Company details

Settings → Company. Phone, email, WhatsApp, address and bank details. These appear on every quote and invoice PDF, on the tracking page and in the customer portal.

3Products with colours & sizes

Products → Product library → New product. Type the colours and sizes; every combination becomes its own stock item with an automatic SKU (e.g. SL-TSH-001-BLA-M).

4Opening stock with a purchase order

Purchasing → Purchase orders → New, place it, then Receive goods. Stock goes up per colour and size, and the unit cost is recorded so profit is accurate later.

5Quote and PDF

A website request arrives as a lead. From the lead, Create quote: ready-stock tees plus made-to-order embroidered polos, with internal costs (never shown to the customer). Send quote gives you a customer link and Download PDF gives you a branded quotation.

6Customer approves from their phone

The customer opens the link — no login. They can download the PDF and approve, decline or request changes. Seamline shows the quote as Viewed as soon as they open it and Approved the moment they approve.

7Order, invoice PDF and live tracking link

Convert to order → Confirm order. Ready stock is reserved, the invoice is issued (Invoice PDF downloads it) and the order gets a secure live tracking link. Send it once by WhatsApp, email or QR code.

8Updating the customer as you go

Every production and delivery status change, every payment and every update you post (with “Show on the customer’s live tracking link” ticked) appears on the same link automatically. The page checks for changes on its own and flashes a “Your order has been updated” message.

9Delivery, payment and profit

QC passed → Ready → Packed → Dispatched → Delivered. Dispatch deducts the stock. Once delivered and fully paid the order completes, the products go into the customer’s reorder library, and Finance → Profit shows revenue, cost and profit split by business type.

10Customer portal and quote request form

Enable the portal from a customer’s profile. They see orders with live status, quotes, invoice PDFs, payments, their product library and one-click reorders. quote-request.html is the public form for your website and Instagram bio — submissions become leads.

12Going live with Supabase

This demo ran in local mode (one browser). To use Seamline across computers and send links customers can open anywhere:

  1. Create a project at supabase.com.
  2. SQL editor → paste supabase/schema.sql → Run.
  3. Project settings → API: copy the URL and the anon public key into js/config.js.
  4. Host the folder on a public address (Netlify, Vercel, GitHub Pages…) and put that address in PUBLIC_BASE_URL.
  5. Open it and create the admin account. Add your team under Settings → Users.
